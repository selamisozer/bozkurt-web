01 Concrete Technologies

Concrete pumps and
concrete delivery technologies

Since 2008, it has been producing truck-mounted concrete pumps, mixer pumps and truck-mounted line pumps at its 70,000 m² production facility in Torbalı, İzmir. Hydraulic and control systems developed at the R&D Design Centre deliver high pumping capacity and long reach.

Betonstar exports almost all of its production and operates through dealer and service networks in more than 50 countries, including the USA, Germany, France, Poland, Spain and Belgium.

02 Construction Equipment

Backhoe loaders
construction equipment

The backhoe loader series developed for demanding job-site and infrastructure projects combines a reinforced chassis architecture, high hydraulic efficiency and operator comfort in a single machine.

Telescopic boom options and fast service support offer wide application flexibility. Excavation, loading, hauling and levelling can all be carried out efficiently with a single machine, even in confined spaces.
