Company

Through its investments in industrial production, BOZKURT-BF brings together two brands in concrete technologies and construction equipment under the same engineering culture.

Milestones

The road to today,
the plan for tomorrow.

The steps from the formation of the group structure to today, followed by the roadmap we have drawn for the period ahead.

Milestone

Brand
  1. 01 Founded

    Founding of BOZKURT-BF

    The foundation of an investment vision focused on industrial production was laid. The group set its strategy of acquiring brands with value-added production and strong engineering know-how.

  2. 02 Acquisition 2025

    Betonstar joined the group

    Betonstar, which has been producing concrete pumps in Torbalı, İzmir since 2008, joined BOZKURT-BF. The brand entered a comprehensive transformation process in product development, corporate identity and sales organisation.

  3. 03 Expansion

    Construction equipment with Fermec

    The Fermec brand in the backhoe loader segment joined the group. The product range thus expanded from concrete technologies to construction equipment; the two brands began to share a common production and service infrastructure.

  4. 04 Today

    Two brands, shared production strength

    Betonstar and Fermec operate with a 70,000 m² integrated production campus, a shared quality standard and a sales network spanning more than 50 countries.

  5. 05 Ahead

    New markets, a wider product range

    Increasing production capacity, expanding the export network and developing new product families are among our priority items.
