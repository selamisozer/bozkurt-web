Company

With its investments in industrial production, it brings together two brands in concrete technologies and construction machinery under the same engineering culture.

About

BOZKURT BF,

carries out its activities in the construction machinery sector with its BETONSTAR and FERMEC brands.

BOZKURT BF offers comprehensive solutions for the needs of different sectors.

Operating in Türkiye and international markets through its extensive dealer network, BOZKURT BF exports to more than 50 countries, including the USA, Germany, France and Poland.

With its BETONSTAR and FERMEC brands, BOZKURT BF continues to strengthen its position in domestic and international markets through its engineering expertise, R&D work and manufacturing strength.

Milestone

Brand
  1. 01 Founded

    Founding of BOZKURT-BF

    The foundation of an investment vision focused on industrial production was laid. The group set its strategy of acquiring brands with value-added production and strong engineering know-how.

  2. 02 Acquisition 2025

    Betonstar joined the group

    Since 2008, Betonstar, which has been producing concrete pumps in Torbalı, İzmir, joined BOZKURT-BF. The brand entered a comprehensive transformation process in product development, corporate identity and sales organization.

  3. 03 Expansion

    Construction equipment with Fermec

    The Fermec brand in the backhoe loader segment joined the group. Thus, the product range expanded from concrete technologies to construction machinery; the two brands began to share a common production and service infrastructure.

  4. 04 Today

    Two brands, shared production strength

    Betonstar and Fermec operate with a 70,000 m² integrated production campus, common quality standard and a sales network reaching more than 50 countries.

  5. 05 Ahead

    New markets, a wider product range

    Increasing production capacity, expanding the export network and developing new product families are among our priority items.
