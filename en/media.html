Press Releases
12 March 2025 Company Betonstar joined BOZKURT-BF

Betonstar, which has been producing concrete pumps in Torbalı, İzmir since 2008, joined BOZKURT-BF. With the acquisition, the brand entered a comprehensive transformation process in product development, corporate identity and sales organisation.

Following the investment, BOZKURT-BF decided to strengthen capacity and R&D infrastructure at its integrated production campus in Torbalı.

2025 Brand Entry into construction equipment with Fermec

With the Fermec brand joining the group in the backhoe loader segment, the product range expanded from concrete technologies to construction equipment. Fermec carries out export-oriented production in the İzmir Free Zone (İZBAŞ).

The two brands began to share a common quality standard, service network and procurement infrastructure.

2025 Production 70,000 m² integrated production campus

The campus in Torbalı brings metal processing, hydraulic assembly, painting and test lines under one roof. Production planning is carried out together with service records from the field.

2025 Export Sales network reached more than 50 countries

Betonstar and Fermec products are sold through dealer structures in European, Middle Eastern, North African and Central Asian markets. Export operations are managed together with spare parts and field service.

Information in the releases reflects the situation at the date of publication. For current data, we recommend confirmation with the press contact team.
